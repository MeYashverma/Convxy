01 · what it does

    
A player that feels like glass.

    
Every surface is frosted, refractive and bouncy — iOS-style scrolling, progressive blur chrome and animations that never drop a frame.

    

      
🫧
Liquid Glass UI
Real backdrop blur & refraction, Material 3 Expressive shapes, custom wordmark typography.

      
🎤
Multi-singer lyrics
Apple Music-style duets: per-singer accent colors and “who is singing” badges, attributed correctly even when the feature artist sings first.
TTML · v1/v2 AGENTS

      
✨
Glow lyrics effect
A breathing accent glow on the active line, consistent across every animation style and fully toggleable.

      
🎵
Word-by-word sync
Karaoke fill, Apple / Vivi / Metro / lyric-V2 styles with per-word timings from Paxsenix, BetterLyrics & Musixmatch.

      
📡
Sources everywhere
YouTube Music streaming, JioSaavn, local files, Spotify/Last.fm metadata & scrobbling.

      
⬇️
Offline downloads
Cache songs, albums and playlists for fully offline listening.

      
🎮
Discord Rich Presence
Share what you’re listening with one toggle — powered by KizzyRPC.

      
🎚️
Equalizer & canvases
Built-in EQ, artist video canvases and ambient visualizers behind the player.

      
🚗
Android Auto
Full playback controls on your car display, plus a tablet-optimized layout.

      
👥
Listen Together
Shared playback sessions with friends over the built-in sync server.

      
Your look
Dynamic theming, icon picker, brand font toggle and expressive accents.

      
🔒
No telemetry
Open source under GPL-3.0. Your library stays on your device.
