02 — Lyrics as an experience

Feel every word land.

Convxy supports word-by-word karaoke, translation, romanisation and progressive blur. In multi-singer lyrics, vocalists get their own colour and name badge — shared parts are treated as a chorus.

Liquid Glass, made from the scene behind it.

Convxy's nav bar, mini player, sheets and dialogs sample their actual backdrop. Blur, refraction, vibrancy, tint and depth are part of the system — not just a translucent surface.

↗
Music becomes video when you want it to.

Watch a track's YouTube video from the player, or move into the native YouTube section for home, search, channels, playlists, Shorts, watch history and related videos in the same queue.

03 — Ambient Mode

Give the music some room.

A sparse landscape display for a TV, desktop or phone stand: artwork and synced lyrics share the frame while a track's glow — or animated canvas — fills the background.

Not another screen.
A listening space.

Tap to play or pause, swipe between tracks, and scrub a progress ring from the edge. Controls disappear when the music has the room.

04 — Sound, considered

More control. Less clutter.

Shape the next transition.

Waveform seeking, a built-in equalizer, offline downloads and per-source quality settings stay close to playback.

Crossfade can move into tempo-matched Auto-DJ mixing when tracks fit — otherwise it falls back to a plain crossfade.

05 — Privacy

Your music stays yours.

Your library, history, settings, downloads and caches live on your device. Convxy includes no analytics or advertising SDK. Optional services only connect after you choose and configure them.

THE ROUTE IS YOURS TO CHOOSE

Your device
Music & lyrics services you use

Network requests are for the services in use, artwork and update checks — not central Convxy analytics.

06 — Under the surface

Designed like a serious music player.

Convxy is a Kotlin, Jetpack Compose multi-module Android application. Its parts stay deliberately separate, so the player, providers and integrations can evolve without becoming one monolith.

Jetpack ComposeProduct UI and interaction
Media3 / ExoPlayerService-based playback
RoomLibrary, history and local state
InnerTube + JioSaavnMusic and video sources
Lyrics modulesProviders, parsing and translation
Canvas providersArtwork motion and Ambient backgrounds
Connected, opt-inDiscord, Last.fm, ListenBrainz, Spotify
Listen TogetherShared queue rooms
