01 · the idea

    
Glass you can see through.

    
Every surface samples the pixels behind it, then blurs and refracts them in real time. Not a flat overlay — actual backdrop capture, the same trick Convx pioneered, kept and pushed further here.

    

      

        

          
The look

          
Liquid Glass

          
Nav bar, sheets and floating buttons all share one backdrop. iOS-style rubber-band overscroll, springy motion, Material You pulled from the current artwork.

          
    
            
  • Real-time backdrop blur & refraction
    • 
            
  • Bouncy overscroll and blurred page transitions
    • 
            
  • Per-element blur / vibrancy / lens dials
    • 
          

        

        

          
The words

          
Multi-singer lyrics

          
Apple Music-style duets: each vocalist gets a color and a “who is singing” badge. Agents inherit from the verse when a line forgets to stamp one, so a featured artist who sings first is never painted as the lead by accident.

          
    
            
  • Word-by-word karaoke fill
    • 
            
  • Breathing accent glow on the active line, every style
    • 
            
  • TTML v1/v2 agents + shared vocals
    • 
          

        

      

      

        

          
The sound

          
Stream & download

          
The whole YouTube Music catalogue, ad-free, plus JioSaavn, local files and optional lossless. Cache anything. Built-in equalizer.

          
    
            
  • Background playback + lock-screen controls
    • 
            
  • Offline downloads with smart storage
    • 
            
  • Android Auto and a tablet sidebar layout
    • 
          

        

        

          
The fine print

          
Zero telemetry

          
No trackers, no analytics, no account required. Your library and preferences never leave the device. In-app updater, no store.

          
    
            
  • 100% local library
    • 
            
  • Open source under GPL-3.0
    • 
            
  • Discord RPC is opt-in, token stays on device
    • 
          

        

      

    

  






  

    
02 · what it does

    
A player that feels like glass.

    
The bits that made the fork worth making, plus everything Convx already did well.

    

      
🫧
Liquid Glass UI
Real backdrop blur & refraction, Material 3 Expressive shapes, custom Chillax wordmark.

      
🎤
Multi-singer lyrics
Per-singer colors and badges. Names come from Apple’s registry, then fuzzy-matched to the track artists — never guessed from v1/v2 position.
TTML · v1/v2 AGENTS

      
✨
Glow, every style
A breathing accent glow on the active line. Honors the same toggle across Apple, Vivi, Metro, Karaoke, Fade and V2.

      
🎵
Word-by-word sync
Karaoke fill from Paxsenix, BetterLyrics and Musixmatch word timings.

      
📡
Sources everywhere
YouTube Music, JioSaavn, local files, Spotify / Last.fm metadata & scrobbling.

      
⬇️
Offline downloads
Cache songs, albums and playlists for fully offline listening.

      
🎮
Discord Rich Presence
Share what you’re listening with one toggle — powered by KizzyRPC.

      
🎚️
Equalizer & canvases
Built-in EQ, artist video canvases and ambient visualizers behind the player.

      
🚗
Android Auto
Full playback controls on your car display, plus a tablet-optimized layout.

      
👥
Listen Together
Shared playback sessions with friends over the built-in sync server.

      
🎨
Your look
Dynamic theming, icon picker, brand font toggle and expressive accents.

      
🔒
No telemetry
Open source under GPL-3.0. Your library stays on your device.
